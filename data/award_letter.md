# Westbrook State University (fictional): SAMPLE 2026–27 Award Letter

Each numbered line has an id (L1, L2, ...) that Beacon cites. Amounts are illustrative.

- L1 SAMPLE award letter. Westbrook State University is a fictional school, and every amount here is illustrative.
- L2 Student: Daniel, an incoming first-year, dependent student. Award year: 2026–27.
- L3 Cost of attendance (COA), the estimated full cost of one year: $38,000 total.
- L4 COA: tuition and fees $14,000.
- L5 COA: housing and meals $16,500.
- L6 COA: books and supplies $1,500.
- L7 COA: transportation $2,000.
- L8 COA: personal expenses $4,000.
- L9 Total aid package offered: $31,500. The package is a mix of grants, loans, and work-study.
- L10 Westbrook Grant (institutional grant from the university): $9,000. Free money; it does not need to be repaid.
- L11 Federal Pell Grant: $6,000. Free money; it does not need to be repaid.
- L12 Federal Direct Subsidized Loan: $3,500. A loan to Daniel; it must be repaid.
- L13 Federal Direct Unsubsidized Loan: $2,000. A loan to Daniel; it must be repaid, and interest accrues while he is in school.
- L14 Federal Work-Study: $2,500. Earned through a campus job and paid as paychecks; it is not credited to the bill upfront.
- L15 Federal Direct Parent PLUS Loan: $8,500. Offered to the parent; optional; requires a separate application and a credit check; repaid by the parent.
- L16 Summary: grants (free money) total $15,000.
- L17 Summary: loans total $14,000 (Subsidized $3,500 + Unsubsidized $2,000 + Parent PLUS $8,500).
- L18 Summary: work-study total $2,500.
- L19 Summary: net price (cost of attendance minus grants) is $23,000.
- L20 Summary: remaining cost after accepting all aid offered is $6,500.
- L21 Requirement: Daniel has been selected for verification. Submit the requested documents (for example, tax return transcripts and a verification worksheet) by July 15, or aid may be delayed or canceled.
- L22 Requirement: the Westbrook Grant requires Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP): at least a 2.0 GPA and completion of at least 67% of attempted credits each term.
- L23 Requirement: accept or decline each award in the student portal.
